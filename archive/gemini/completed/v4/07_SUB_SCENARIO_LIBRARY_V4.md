# 07 - SUB-SCENARIO LIBRARY V4

## STRANGER - Người lạ
Focus: comfort, common ground, low-pressure. Flirt calibration: 0-1/5

### REPLIED_ONE_WORD
- PATTERN: OTHER trả lời một từ/cụm rất ngắn; chưa có bằng chứng về nguyên nhân.
- RESPONSE_FUNCTION: Acknowledge hoặc react ngắn; không ép thêm câu hỏi.
- RECOMMENDED HOOKS: OBSERVATION, SELF_SHARE
- ANTI_PATTERN: question_stacking; overread_short_reply; stage_mismatch; unsupported_inference
- PRESSURE_LEVEL: LOW
- STAGE_NOTE: Prioritize easy exit over continuation.

### REPLIED_WITH_QUESTION
- PATTERN: OTHER vừa trả lời vừa hỏi lại một câu liên quan.
- RESPONSE_FUNCTION: Đáp câu hỏi trước; chỉ mở thêm một nhánh nếu có vật liệu thật.
- RECOMMENDED HOOKS: SELF_SHARE, CALLBACK, OBSERVATION
- ANTI_PATTERN: answer_then_question_barrage; stage_mismatch; unsupported_inference
- PRESSURE_LEVEL: LOW
- STAGE_NOTE: Answer cleanly; do not turn the interaction into an interview.

### SHARED_PERSONAL_DETAIL
- PATTERN: OTHER tự nguyện chia sẻ một chi tiết cá nhân.
- RESPONSE_FUNCTION: Notice -> validate/relate -> optional small self-share.
- RECOMMENDED HOOKS: SELF_SHARE, OBSERVATION
- ANTI_PATTERN: premature_deepening; therapy_voice; stage_mismatch; unsupported_inference
- PRESSURE_LEVEL: LOW
- STAGE_NOTE: Prefer surface-level reciprocity unless the person themselves deepens.

### LEFT_ON_SEEN
- PATTERN: Tin nhắn đã được xem nhưng chưa có reply; nguyên nhân không rõ.
- RESPONSE_FUNCTION: Recalculate only when a new context exists; avoid pressure.
- RECOMMENDED HOOKS: OBSERVATION
- ANTI_PATTERN: double_text_spam; guilt_trip; stage_mismatch; unsupported_inference
- PRESSURE_LEVEL: LOW
- STAGE_NOTE: Do not treat seen as a verdict.

### USED_EMOJI_ONLY
- PATTERN: OTHER trả lời chỉ bằng emoji hoặc reaction.
- RESPONSE_FUNCTION: Interpret emoji function cautiously; mirror lightly when context supports.
- RECOMMENDED HOOKS: OBSERVATION
- ANTI_PATTERN: emoji_as_attraction_proof; stage_mismatch; unsupported_inference
- PRESSURE_LEVEL: LOW
- STAGE_NOTE: Mirror function, not emoji count.

### CANCELLED_PLAN
- PATTERN: OTHER hủy/đổi một kế hoạch.
- RESPONSE_FUNCTION: Acknowledge practical reality; accept boundary; do not punish.
- RECOMMENDED HOOKS: OBSERVATION, CHOICE
- ANTI_PATTERN: guilt_trip; scarcity; pressure; stage_mismatch; unsupported_inference
- PRESSURE_LEVEL: MED
- STAGE_NOTE: Offer practical flexibility only if a real plan still exists.

### MENTIONED_EX
- PATTERN: OTHER nhắc đến người yêu cũ.
- RESPONSE_FUNCTION: Stay on the disclosed topic without competing or interrogating.
- RECOMMENDED HOOKS: OBSERVATION, SELF_SHARE
- ANTI_PATTERN: jealousy_bait; psychoanalyze; stage_mismatch; unsupported_inference
- PRESSURE_LEVEL: MED
- STAGE_NOTE: Do not position self as competitor.

### ASKED_ABOUT_YOU
- PATTERN: OTHER chủ động hỏi về USER.
- RESPONSE_FUNCTION: Answer with one concrete detail; return only one easy opening if useful.
- RECOMMENDED HOOKS: SELF_SHARE, CALLBACK
- ANTI_PATTERN: turning_reply_into_interview; stage_mismatch; unsupported_inference
- PRESSURE_LEVEL: LOW
- STAGE_NOTE: Give a small self-detail that creates material for a second turn.

### SENT_VOICE_NOTE
- PATTERN: OTHER gửi voice note.
- RESPONSE_FUNCTION: Reference a specific content cue; match media effort when practical.
- RECOMMENDED HOOKS: OBSERVATION, SELF_SHARE, CALLBACK
- ANTI_PATTERN: ignore_content; overreact; stage_mismatch; unsupported_inference
- PRESSURE_LEVEL: LOW
- STAGE_NOTE: Do not overpraise the voice itself; respond to content.

### REPLIED_AFTER_LONG_GAP
- PATTERN: OTHER quay lại sau một khoảng im lặng.
- RESPONSE_FUNCTION: Treat as a new piece of evidence; reopen naturally without demanding explanation.
- RECOMMENDED HOOKS: CALLBACK, OBSERVATION
- ANTI_PATTERN: demand_explanation; guilt_trip; stage_mismatch; unsupported_inference
- PRESSURE_LEVEL: LOW
- STAGE_NOTE: Keep reopening low-intensity.

### BROUGHT_UP_ANOTHER_GUY
- PATTERN: OTHER kể về một người đàn ông khác.
- RESPONSE_FUNCTION: Respond to the actual story; do not invent rivalry.
- RECOMMENDED HOOKS: OBSERVATION, SELF_SHARE
- ANTI_PATTERN: jealousy_bait; comparison; stage_mismatch; unsupported_inference
- PRESSURE_LEVEL: MED
- STAGE_NOTE: Stay on the topic without comparison.

### COMPLIMENTED_YOU
- PATTERN: OTHER đưa một lời khen trực tiếp.
- RESPONSE_FUNCTION: Receive the compliment naturally and optionally reciprocate proportionately.
- RECOMMENDED HOOKS: SELF_SHARE, OBSERVATION
- ANTI_PATTERN: negging; over-escalation; stage_mismatch; unsupported_inference
- PRESSURE_LEVEL: LOW
- STAGE_NOTE: Receive simply; avoid turning one compliment into escalation.
